Figure 2. Equilibrium allocation of feedstock among the three agents against availability, under linear operating cost. Agents fill in strict merit order of net margin m_i: STRAP absorbs everything up to its own 6,000 t/yr capacity, incineration then absorbs the next 4,000 t/yr, and gasification never enters because m_G < 0. Beyond 10,000 t/yr the gap between the stacked total and the 45-degree line is feedstock no agent buys, which is landfilled.
