Figure 1. Equilibrium shadow price of shared feedstock, lambda, against total feedstock availability Q_tot, under linear operating cost. lambda is piecewise CONSTANT: whichever agent is on the margin has a constant marginal cost, so it holds one price for as long as it is marginal. The price steps down each time the marginal agent saturates -- at Q_tot = Cap_S = 6,000 t/yr, where the marginal buyer switches from STRAP to incineration, and at Q_tot = Cap_S + Cap_W = 10,000 t/yr, where feedstock becomes abundant and lambda falls to zero. Gasification never enters at any Q_tot because its net margin is negative.
